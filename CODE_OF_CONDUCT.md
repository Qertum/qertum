# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, accept feedback gracefully, and do not harass or demean anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules and may ban people who do so repeatedly.

To report unacceptable behaviour, email **security@qertum.com** with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled in confidence.
